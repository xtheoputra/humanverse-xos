## §29 — Explainable AI

> AI harus mampu menjelaskan: *"Mengapa kamu menyarankan ini?"*

Contoh:

```
Recommendation: Sleep earlier

Reasons:
1. Sleep decreased 18% this week
2. Workout completion decreased
3. Focus score decreased
4. Similar pattern occurred last month
```

> Ini jauh lebih dipercaya daripada *"AI thinks you should sleep."*

---

## §30 — Human Insight Engine

> Setiap hari AI mencari insight.

```
Insight of the Day

"Pada 4 dari 5 hari ketika kamu tidur sebelum 23:30,
 workout-mu berhasil dilakukan."
```

> Itu sangat valuable.
